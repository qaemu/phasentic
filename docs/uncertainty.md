# Uncertainty and decision language

The alpha score is not a probability. For Cu Kα, exploratory profile v0.8 ranks candidates using peak-position agreement (25%), measured-intensity-weighted coverage of the ten strongest observed peaks (35%), relative-intensity agreement among matched top-ten peaks (10%), and intensity-weighted coverage of the ten strongest predicted reflections (30%). The observed-peak coverage term uses corrected peak heights as weights; the separate reference-reflection term checks whether a candidate explains its own strongest expected lines and may be affected by preferred orientation, overlap, or acquisition range. Benchmark precision is a different metric: the fraction of predicted phases that agree with the accepted reference set. A candidate is `supported` only under the provisional boundary in `PARAMETERS.md`; it still requires scientist review.

Reports must disclose missing metadata, calibration assumptions, incomplete reference coverage, background dominance, preferred orientation risk, peak overlap, and whether POW_COD or only the demo subset was used. Use “top hypothesis” and “candidate” until a benchmark establishes calibrated performance for the relevant instrument and sample class.
